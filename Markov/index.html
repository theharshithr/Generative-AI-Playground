🧠 How Markov Chains Work

A Markov chain is a mathematical system that transitions from one state to another based on probability. In text generation, each word (or sequence of words) is a "state," and the model learns the probability of what word comes next. The "order" determines how many previous words influence the next word choice. Higher order = more coherent but less creative text.
